Cast your phone to the TV

Phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi. Pick your phone and your TV.

Watch with your phone's NordVPN

  1. Open the NordVPN app on the phone and connect to the country you want.
  2. Start the show in the phone's browser or app.
  3. Mirror the screen with the steps above. The TV shows what the phone streams, so it comes through your phone's VPN.
Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and some other apps black out the picture when you mirror the whole screen (copy protection). For those, use TV VPN → Gateway so the TV's own app goes through NordVPN.

Give the TV NordVPN

Samsung and LG TVs cannot run a VPN app, so the VPN has to come from outside the TV. Three ways, best first.

1. Linked Buddy Gateway Best

A Raspberry Pi (or old Linux PC) runs NordVPN for the TV. Plug the TV's network cable into it, or point the TV at it. Everything on the TV goes through NordVPN, including Netflix and other apps. If NordVPN drops, the TV loses internet instead of showing your real location.

2. NordVPN SmartDNS No extra box

Changes only the TV's DNS. Unblocks many streaming catalogs, but it does not encrypt or hide your IP address.

  1. On a phone or computer on the same home Wi-Fi as the TV, sign in at my.nordaccount.com → NordVPN → Set up SmartDNS → Activate. This registers your home IP address.
  2. Type these on the TV:
    Primary DNS 103.86.96.103
    Secondary DNS 103.86.99.103
Samsung
  1. Settings → General → Network → Network Status
  2. IP Settings → DNS Settings → Enter manually
  3. Type the primary DNS → OK, then restart the TV (hold Power until it reboots).
LG webOS
  1. Settings → All Settings → Network
  2. Wired or Wi-Fi Connection → your network → Advanced / Edit
  3. Untick Set Automatically, type both DNS addresses → Connect.
Your home IP can change. If streaming stops unlocking, press Activate again in your Nord Account.

3. Mirror from the phone Quickest

Connect NordVPN on the phone and mirror it to the TV (see Cast). Works for websites and most apps; protected apps may show a black screen.

A phone hotspot does not pass the phone's VPN to the TV on most iPhones and Android phones, so mirroring or the Gateway are the reliable ways.

Streaming services' terms may forbid using a VPN to change region, and some detect and block VPN servers. Check the terms of the services you use.

Linked Buddy Gateway

Turns a Raspberry Pi 3/4/5 or any Debian/Ubuntu PC into the TV's NordVPN box.

What you need

  • Raspberry Pi with Raspberry Pi OS, joined to your home Wi-Fi (so its Ethernet port is free for the TV), or a PC with two network ports.
  • A network cable from the Pi to the TV.
  • Your NordVPN service credentials: my.nordaccount.com → NordVPN → Manual setup. These are different from your email and password.

Install (about 5 minutes)

  1. Copy the gateway folder from the Linked Buddy download to the Pi (USB stick or scp).
  2. On the Pi open Terminal in that folder and run:
    sudo bash install.sh
  3. Choose 1 (Cable into the Pi), type the NordVPN service login, a control-page password and the country.
  4. Plug the TV into the Pi. On the TV pick Wired network with automatic settings. Done.

Check and control your Gateway

Use the control page address shown at the end of the install (the Pi's address on your home Wi-Fi, for example 192.168.1.40).

Open control page

The control page asks for the password you chose at install (any user name). From there you switch country, turn the VPN on or off, and see the exact TV settings.

Linked Buddy on the TV

A small TV app that shows, on the TV itself, which country the TV appears to be in and whether it is going through your Gateway. Pick it from the TV's app list after installing.

Samsung (Tizen)

  1. On the TV open Apps, press 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote, turn Developer mode On and enter your computer's IP address. Restart the TV.
  2. On the computer install Tizen Studio with the TV Extensions and create a free Samsung certificate (Certificate Manager).
  3. Import the tv/samsung-tizen folder, then Run As → Tizen Web Application with the TV selected in Device Manager.

LG (webOS)

  1. Install Developer Mode from the LG Content Store, sign in with a free LG developer account and switch Dev Mode Status On.
  2. Install the webOS CLI (npm i -g @webos-tools/cli), add the TV with ares-setup-device.
  3. In tv/lg-webos: ares-package . && ares-install *.ipk

TV makers do not let apps change network settings or run a VPN, so the TV app reports status. The Gateway and SmartDNS steps are what change where the TV appears to be.
